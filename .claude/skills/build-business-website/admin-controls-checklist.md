# Admin controls checklist

Go through every group. Include the items the business model needs. For each included item note the role allowed and whether it is audit-logged.

## Catalog and content
- Create, edit, duplicate, archive, schedule and bulk-edit products, services, plans or listings. Variants, options, images, descriptions, SEO fields.
- Categories, tags, collections, featured items, ordering.
- Pages, blog or portfolio items, banners and announcements, media library, redirects, legal pages.
- Draft, preview and publish states.

## Pricing and promotions
- Prices per currency, sale prices with start and end dates, tax rules, shipping rates and zones.
- Coupons and discount codes: percentage, fixed, free shipping, usage limits, expiry, per-customer limits, stacking rules.
- Plans, trials, intro offers.

## Inventory, availability and booking (if relevant)
- Stock levels, low-stock alerts, backorders, out-of-stock behaviour.
- Calendar, capacity, working hours, blackout dates, buffers, cancellation windows.

## Orders and fulfilment
- Order list with search, filters and export. Status workflow with allowed transitions.
- Edit, cancel, full and partial refund, resend confirmation, add internal notes, attach tracking, print invoice or packing slip.
- Manual order creation. Fraud or risk flags.

## Subscriptions (if relevant)
- View, pause, resume, cancel now or at period end, change plan, proration, apply credit, extend trial.
- Failed-payment handling: retry schedule, customer emails, grace period, automatic suspension.
- MRR, churn and plan mix.

## Customers
- Search, profile, order and payment history, notes, tags, segments.
- Suspend or ban, force password reset, verify email manually.
- Impersonate a customer to reproduce a problem, with a visible banner and an audit entry.
- Export or delete personal data on request.

## Payments and finance
- Payment list, reconciliation with Stripe, disputes and evidence, payout view, tax reports, refund reasons.
- Webhook log with failures and a safe retry.

## Marketplace and vendors (only if the model has sellers)
- Seller onboarding and identity checks, approval, suspension.
- Listing moderation queue, commission rules, payout schedule and holds, seller disputes, reports per seller.

## Communication and support
- Email templates with preview and test send, transactional and marketing separated, unsubscribe handling.
- Contact form inbox, support tickets or a link to the support tool, review and comment moderation.
- Admin alerts: new order, failed payment, low stock, dispute, error spike.

## Marketing and growth
- SEO fields per page, sitemap control, redirects, analytics and campaign tracking, abandoned-cart or win-back messages, referral or affiliate if relevant.

## Staff and access
- Staff accounts, roles and permissions editor, forced 2FA, session list and revoke, IP or login alerts.
- Audit log: who did what, when, on which record, with before and after values. Not editable.

## Settings and operations
- Business details, tax registration, currencies, shipping origin, email sender, payment settings.
- Feature flags per site, maintenance mode, per-site branding if there is more than one site.
- Bulk actions, CSV import and export, scheduled jobs and their status.
- Backups, restore instructions, error and uptime links.

## Reports and dashboard
- Revenue, orders, average order value, conversion, top products or pages, new versus returning customers, refunds, subscription metrics, traffic sources. Date range and export on each.
